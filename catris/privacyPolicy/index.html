Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Catris is a casual puzzle game developed by Woldra. Your privacy is important to us, and we believe in keeping things simple.

This Privacy Policy explains how Catris handles information when you play the game.

2. Information Collection

Catris does not collect, transmit, or share personal information or user data.

The game does not:

  • Require users to create an account or provide personal information.
  • Collect personal information, device information, or location data.
  • Use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
  • Send gameplay activity or other information to external servers.

Any gameplay data or preferences stored by the game remain locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

3. Third-Party Services

Catris does not use third-party services to collect or process personal information.

4. Data Security and Retention

Since Catris does not collect or transmit personal information, we do not maintain databases of user information or retain personal data on external servers.

Any locally stored game data can be removed by uninstalling the application or clearing its app data through your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Catris does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The game does not require users to provide personal information to play.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
